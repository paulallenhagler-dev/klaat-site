00 / THE SOUND CAME FIRST

Before there was an atlas, there was a breath held against a hollow column of agave.

A drum entered. One strike divided the sound into a before and an after. Another strike made the interval imaginable.

With the third, the ear could begin waiting for the fourth.

The Atlas begins in music and moves through acoustics, perception, body, memory and the stubborn difference between what an instrument produces and what a listener experiences. It is an art book with a research foundation—not a claim that one pitch, one rhythm, or one technique has universal power.
