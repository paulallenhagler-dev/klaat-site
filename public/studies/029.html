AUDITORY ADAPTATION: CONTINUOUS DRONE CAN CHANGE WITHOUT CHANGING

Independent research session from the KLAAT Resonance Research corpus.

This early session is an archival reconstruction, not a verbatim transcript. Its status is preserved exactly as marked in the source corpus.

Core lesson When Amastacia remains stable, the auditory system increasingly treats that stability as background. A small later change can become highly salient because the listener has learned the regularity.

Mismatch negativity is one laboratory example of neural sensitivity to deviations within regular sound sequences, though a live KLAAT experience is much more complex.

Evidence boundary Established: auditory systems adapt to regularities and respond strongly to deviations. Plausible: subtle KLAAT timbral changes can become more salient after prolonged stability than in a constantly ornamented passage.

KLAAT test Compare the same five-second timbral alteration in two contexts: after several minutes of extremely stable drone and after several minutes of continuously changing drone. Ask which alteration felt larger.
