Amastacia can potentially control roughness through vocalization, embouchure, overtone structure, and spectral instability. This makes roughness a separate compositional axis from loudness or BPM.

Evidence boundary Established: close spectral interactions and rapid amplitude fluctuations contribute to roughness and aspects of sensory dissonance. Supported but not universal: rougher sounds can feel more urgent or aversive. Unsupported: calling any roughness band a universal “fear frequency.”

KLAAT test Record three matched-loudness Amastacia passages: spectrally stable, moderately rough, and intentionally rough. Compare listener ratings for tension, activation, urgency, and loudness.
