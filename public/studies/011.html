Two passages at the same BPM can therefore feel radically different if one uses rounded attacks and the other sharp attacks. Perceived speed and urgency can change without changing the metronomic rate.

Evidence boundary Established: onset timing contributes strongly to beat perception and auditory-motor prediction. Plausible: sharper Ankora attacks may increase activation or temporal precision at equal BPM.

KLAAT test Keep one constant pulse and move through rounded attacks → normal attacks → sharply articulated attacks → rounded again. Have listeners rate urgency and perceived speed without being told the BPM never changed.
