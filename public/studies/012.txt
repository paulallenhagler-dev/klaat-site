SESSION 12 — BONE CONDUCTION & PERFORMER VS RECEIVER
ARCHIVAL RECONSTRUCTION — NOT VERBATIM
Core lesson
The performer does not hear Amastacia the same way the audience does. The player receives airborne sound plus internally conducted vibration through the jaw, skull, chest, hands, and instrument contact. The listener receives a different combination dominated by air, room reflections, and whatever mechanical coupling reaches the body.


This means Paul’s embodied perception while playing cannot be treated as a proxy for the audience experience.


Evidence boundary
Established: bone conduction contributes to hearing; self-produced sounds are heard through a different sensory mix than externally produced sounds. Unsupported: assuming that because the performer feels powerful vibration, the audience necessarily receives the same intensity or spectrum.


KLAAT test
Perform a five-minute Amastacia passage and record your own bodily ratings immediately afterward. Later listen to a recording from a participant position and compare what disappeared, what became more prominent, and what was impossible to capture.
