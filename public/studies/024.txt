SESSION 24 — BONE CONDUCTION: KLAAT HEARD THROUGH MORE THAN EARS
ARCHIVAL RECONSTRUCTION — NOT VERBATIM


Core lesson
Live KLAAT reaches the listener through multiple pathways: air conduction, bone conduction, and tactile/mechanical vibration. Floor and chair coupling can alter the total experience even when the acoustic signal is similar.


Bone conduction does not bypass hearing risk. Intense sound reaching the inner ear by any route can still contribute to exposure.


Evidence boundary
Established: bone conduction is a real hearing pathway; mechanical coupling alters bodily vibration. Unsupported: treating bone conduction as a safe way to use arbitrarily high levels.


KLAAT test
Compare the same KLAAT passage while standing, sitting on a rigid chair, and sitting on a padded chair. Rate airborne loudness and bodily vibration separately.
