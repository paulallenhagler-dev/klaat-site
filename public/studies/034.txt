SESSION 34 — INTEROCEPTION DEEPER
ARCHIVAL RECONSTRUCTION — NOT VERBATIM


Core lesson
A listener reporting a chest pulse may be sensing heartbeat, breathing motion, acoustic pressure, floor vibration, expectation, or several of these simultaneously. Interoceptive reports therefore require neutral questioning and careful separation from exteroceptive vibration.


Predictive accounts of interoception emphasize that bodily perception is shaped by incoming signals plus prior expectations and interpretation.


Evidence boundary
Established: internal bodily perception is inferential and can be influenced by attention and expectation. Unsupported: labeling a sensation as a specific organ response or “energy movement” without measurement.


KLAAT test
Use two minutes of drone, five minutes of drone plus pulse, then remove Ankora abruptly while keeping Amastacia going. Ask only: “What changed in your body, if anything?” Avoid mentioning heartbeat, chest, vibration, or relaxation beforehand.
