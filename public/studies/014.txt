SESSION 14 — WHY REPETITION CAN ALTER TIME PERCEPTION
ARCHIVAL RECONSTRUCTION — NOT VERBATIM
Core lesson
Subjective time depends on attention, arousal, memory, expectation, and event density. Repetition can create absorption for one listener and boredom for another; there is no single formula that guarantees trance.


KLAAT can nevertheless manipulate perceived duration by changing how much novelty or event structure occurs inside a fixed amount of clock time.


Evidence boundary
Established: prospective and retrospective time judgments differ and are influenced by attention and memory. Supported but variable: repetitive sensory environments can alter perceived duration. Unsupported: claiming a certain number of minutes necessarily induces a defined altered state.


KLAAT test
Perform a 12-minute passage divided into 4 minutes varied, 4 minutes highly stable, and 4 minutes varied again. Ask listeners to estimate total duration and identify which segment felt longest or shortest.
