SESSION 27 — RESONANT MODES: WHY THE ROOM HAS PREFERRED FREQUENCIES
ARCHIVAL RECONSTRUCTION — NOT VERBATIM


Core lesson
Room modes are standing-wave patterns determined by dimensions and boundary conditions. At modal frequencies, some locations can show strong pressure reinforcement while others show cancellations or weak response.


A sustained Amastacia drone is especially good at revealing this because it continuously excites the room.


Evidence boundary
Established: enclosed rooms support resonant modes, nodes, and antinodes. Unsupported: translating a strong room mode into a biological healing claim.


KLAAT test
Hold one stable Amastacia tone and walk the room slowly, including corners, center lines, crouched positions, and standing positions. Mark strong and weak locations to create a resonance map.
