SESSION 19 — RESPIRATORY ENTRAINMENT: CAN DRONE CHANGE BREATHING?
ARCHIVAL RECONSTRUCTION — NOT VERBATIM


Core lesson
A change in breathing rate is not automatically entrainment. True entrainment requires a reproducible temporal relationship between the stimulus and respiration, potentially including phase locking or a stable n:m ratio.


Amastacia may influence breathing through phrasing, attention, relaxation, activation, or imitation of performer breath. Ankora provides a separate rhythmic reference. These effects need to be distinguished.


Evidence boundary
Established: auditory rhythm and attention can influence behavior and timing; breathing rate is modifiable by context. Plausible: some listeners may stabilize breathing relative to KLAAT timing. Unsupported: claiming that a listener has “entrained” merely because respiration slowed.


KLAAT test
Record two minutes of baseline breathing followed by a KLAAT passage. Mark inhalation onsets and Ankora strikes, then look for stable relationships across time rather than simply comparing average breaths per minute.
