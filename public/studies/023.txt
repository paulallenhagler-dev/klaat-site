SESSION 23 — ROUGHNESS: WHY SOME SOUNDS FEEL PHYSICALLY URGENT
ARCHIVAL RECONSTRUCTION — NOT VERBATIM


Core lesson
As interacting frequency components produce faster amplitude fluctuations, the percept can move from slow beating toward roughness. Roughness is a sensory texture related to rapid modulation within auditory filters and can contribute to urgency or aversiveness.


Biological alarm sounds can contain roughness, but that does not make any one roughness rate a universal fear frequency.


Evidence boundary
Established: rapid modulation and close-frequency interactions contribute to roughness. Supported but context-dependent: rough sounds often receive higher urgency or unpleasantness ratings. Unsupported: universal emotional decoding from one roughness band.


KLAAT test
Create pure, moderately rough, and strongly rough Amastacia passages at similar loudness. Randomize playback and collect separate ratings for loudness, tension, activation, bodily intensity, and roughness.
