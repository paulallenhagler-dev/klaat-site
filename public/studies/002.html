KLAAT must distinguish airborne vibration from mechanical coupling. Ankora can reach a listener through air, but it can also excite a floor, chair, or platform, which then transmits motion into the body. Auditory and tactile events can be integrated into one experience even though they arrive through different sensory pathways.

Evidence boundary Established: vibrotactile perception is real and frequency-dependent; different mechanoreceptor channels contribute differently. Context-dependent: therapeutic claims about vibroacoustic interventions vary by protocol and evidence quality.

KLAAT test Compare Ankora at the same playing intensity under two conditions: listener on a rigidly coupled surface versus a more isolated/padded condition. Rate what is heard separately from what is physically felt.
