SESSION 18 — INTEROCEPTION: SAME SOUND, DIFFERENT INTERNAL EXPERIENCE
ARCHIVAL RECONSTRUCTION — NOT VERBATIM


Core lesson
Interoception concerns the perception and interpretation of internal bodily signals such as breathing, heartbeat, pressure, fullness, warmth, and tension. Interoceptive accuracy, attention, and interpretation are not the same thing.


KLAAT may sometimes increase the salience of signals that were already present rather than creating new physiological events. Reduced visual input and sustained attention can shift the balance from outward environmental monitoring toward inward body awareness.


Evidence boundary
Established: people differ in interoceptive attention and accuracy; internal sensations are shaped by both incoming signals and interpretation. Plausible: a stable KLAAT field can redirect attention inward. Unsupported: assuming every reported internal sensation was physically caused by sound.


KLAAT test
Have a listener rate awareness of heartbeat, breathing, chest, abdomen, skin, and muscle tension before and after ten minutes of stable drone plus gentle pulse. Use neutral wording and do not suggest what they “should” feel.
