For KLAAT, silence should be composed rather than treated as empty space. Return Path can use abrupt subtraction as a major event.

Evidence boundary Established: sensory adaptation and predictive processing change how a stimulus is experienced over time; contrast alters salience. Plausible: post-sound silence may increase interoceptive attention in some listeners. Unsupported: interpreting that inward attention as automatic healing or trauma release.

KLAAT test Play Amastacia continuously for about ten minutes, stop abruptly, and remain silent for three minutes. Ask when the experience felt strongest: during the drone, at the cutoff, or in the silence afterward.
