SESSION 15 — HABITUATION: CONSTANT INTENSITY STOPS FEELING INTENSE
ARCHIVAL RECONSTRUCTION — NOT VERBATIM
Core lesson
When stimulation remains unchanged, its subjective impact often diminishes. Adaptation occurs within sensory systems; habituation describes reduced response to repeated non-significant stimulation. A change can restore salience through dishabituation.


KLAAT should therefore avoid spending its strongest material continuously. A useful arc is establish → habituate → violate → reorient.


Evidence boundary
Established: repeated constant stimulation often produces reduced neural or behavioral response; novelty can restore attention. Plausible: a relatively small deviation after long KLAAT stability can feel larger than a constant high-intensity texture.


KLAAT test
Maintain a stable drone-plus-pulse field for about six minutes, then introduce one carefully controlled deviation in timing, timbre, or silence. Ask whether attention snapped back and what feature caused it.
