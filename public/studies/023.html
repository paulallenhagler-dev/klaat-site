ROUGHNESS: WHY SOME SOUNDS FEEL PHYSICALLY URGENT

Biological alarm sounds can contain roughness, but that does not make any one roughness rate a universal fear frequency.

SESSION 023 / 164Evidence & MethodARCHIVAL RECONSTRUCTION · NOT VERBATIM

This early session is an archival reconstruction, not a verbatim transcript. Its status is preserved exactly as marked in the source corpus.

Core lesson As interacting frequency components produce faster amplitude fluctuations, the percept can move from slow beating toward roughness. Roughness is a sensory texture related to rapid modulation within auditory filters and can contribute to urgency or aversiveness.

Biological alarm sounds can contain roughness, but that does not make any one roughness rate a universal fear frequency.

Evidence boundary Established: rapid modulation and close-frequency interactions contribute to roughness. Supported but context-dependent: rough sounds often receive higher urgency or unpleasantness ratings. Unsupported: universal emotional decoding from one roughness band.

KLAAT test Create pure, moderately rough, and strongly rough Amastacia passages at similar loudness. Randomize playback and collect separate ratings for loudness, tension, activation, bodily intensity, and roughness.
