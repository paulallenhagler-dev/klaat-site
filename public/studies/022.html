Core lesson One sound can make another harder to hear. Simultaneous masking occurs when sounds overlap; forward masking can briefly affect perception after the masker stops. Auditory filters and spectral proximity strongly influence how much masking occurs.

Ankora can therefore temporarily hide details in Amastacia, and Amastacia can obscure drum components. More layers do not necessarily reveal more information.

Evidence boundary Established: auditory masking is a core property of hearing and depends on spectrum, level, and timing. Plausible: KLAAT can deliberately compose mask-and-reveal transitions. Unsupported: assuming spectral complexity always increases immersion.

KLAAT test Use the same Amastacia recording beneath soft, moderate, and strong Ankora overdubs. Ask listeners to track one specific harmonic or overtone and identify the point at which it disappears and reappears.
