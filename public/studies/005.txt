SESSION 5 — RHYTHMIC ENTRAINMENT, PREDICTION & TRANCE
ARCHIVAL RECONSTRUCTION — NOT VERBATIM
Core lesson
“Entrainment” is not one thing. KLAAT should distinguish perceptual entrainment, motor synchronization, neural stimulus-locking, and physiological coupling. Strong evidence exists for beat prediction and movement synchronization; claims that every bodily system locks exactly to a musical pulse are much less secure.


Repetition teaches the nervous system what to expect. Once a rule is learned, small deviations become disproportionately meaningful. This creates a useful compositional principle: establish → entrain → violate → resolve.


Evidence boundary
Established: people predict periodic events and often synchronize movement with rhythm. Context-dependent: changes in breathing, heart rate, or autonomic state may accompany rhythmic listening but do not imply exact phase locking. Trance is a broad experiential construct, not one single circuit or frequency.


KLAAT test
Play a simple stable pulse for about five minutes. Near minute four, omit one expected strike. Ask afterward whether the missing event was noticed and whether the silence felt stronger than a normal strike.
