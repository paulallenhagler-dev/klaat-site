KLAAT can nevertheless manipulate perceived duration by changing how much novelty or event structure occurs inside a fixed amount of clock time.

Evidence boundary Established: prospective and retrospective time judgments differ and are influenced by attention and memory. Supported but variable: repetitive sensory environments can alter perceived duration. Unsupported: claiming a certain number of minutes necessarily induces a defined altered state.

KLAAT test Perform a 12-minute passage divided into 4 minutes varied, 4 minutes highly stable, and 4 minutes varied again. Ask listeners to estimate total duration and identify which segment felt longest or shortest.
