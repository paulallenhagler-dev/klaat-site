This early session is an archival reconstruction, not a verbatim transcript. Its status is preserved exactly as marked in the source corpus.

Repetition teaches the nervous system what to expect. Once a rule is learned, small deviations become disproportionately meaningful. This creates a useful compositional principle: establish → entrain → violate → resolve.

Evidence boundary Established: people predict periodic events and often synchronize movement with rhythm. Context-dependent: changes in breathing, heart rate, or autonomic state may accompany rhythmic listening but do not imply exact phase locking. Trance is a broad experiential construct, not one single circuit or frequency.

KLAAT test Play a simple stable pulse for about five minutes. Near minute four, omit one expected strike. Ask afterward whether the missing event was noticed and whether the silence felt stronger than a normal strike.
