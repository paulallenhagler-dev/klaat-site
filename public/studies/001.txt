SESSION 1 — RESONANCE IS NOT “FREQUENCY MAGIC”
ARCHIVAL RECONSTRUCTION — NOT VERBATIM
Core lesson
Resonance is a physical phenomenon in which a system responds strongly when it is driven near one of its natural modes. That does not mean the human body has one magical tuning frequency, nor does a named frequency automatically produce a specific healing effect. KLAAT should separate measurable acoustic resonance from metaphorical or symbolic uses of the word.


A KLAAT event is better understood as an interaction among the didgeridoo fundamental and harmonics, Ankora’s transient spectrum, room resonances, auditory perception, tactile perception, repetition, duration, intensity, spatial position, and expectation. Frequency alone is never enough.


Evidence boundary
Established: mechanical and acoustic systems resonate; low-frequency sound can be physically felt at sufficient level; mechanoreceptors respond to vibration. Unsupported: universal claims that 432 Hz, 528 Hz, or similar frequencies inherently heal the body.


KLAAT test
Play Amastacia from three listening positions and record where the same drone feels strongest, weakest, most audible, and most physical. Treat differences as room/body-position effects unless measurement shows otherwise.
