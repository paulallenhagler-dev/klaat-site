This early session is an archival reconstruction, not a verbatim transcript. Its status is preserved exactly as marked in the source corpus.

For KLAAT, the important question is whether the performance alters breathing without explicit instruction. That could provide one plausible pathway from sound and attention to broader physiological change.

Evidence boundary Established: respiration affects heart-rate dynamics and autonomic measures. Supported but individual: slow breathing often increases certain HRV measures and baroreflex-related oscillations. Unsupported: claiming KLAAT automatically “activates the vagus nerve” in a specific therapeutic way.

KLAAT test Use a sequence of silent baseline, drone, drone-plus-pulse, return to drone, then silence. Count visible breaths or use a consenting participant’s self-count and compare breathing rate across phases.
