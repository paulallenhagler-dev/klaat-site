KLAAT should therefore avoid spending its strongest material continuously. A useful arc is establish → habituate → violate → reorient.

Evidence boundary Established: repeated constant stimulation often produces reduced neural or behavioral response; novelty can restore attention. Plausible: a relatively small deviation after long KLAAT stability can feel larger than a constant high-intensity texture.

KLAAT test Maintain a stable drone-plus-pulse field for about six minutes, then introduce one carefully controlled deviation in timing, timbre, or silence. Ask whether attention snapped back and what feature caused it.
