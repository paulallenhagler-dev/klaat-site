Core lesson True biological entrainment is stronger evidence than simple co-occurrence. Two oscillators can show n:m relationships, meaning several cycles of one process align with one cycle of another. But temporary numerical similarity can occur by chance.

For KLAAT, possible coupled rhythms include performer respiration, Amastacia phrasing, Ankora pulse, listener respiration, and cardiac fluctuations. Each must be measured separately.

Evidence boundary Established: biological rhythms can show phase relationships and n:m synchronization under some conditions. Unsupported: calling any repeated ratio proof of entrainment without testing stability, phase, and what happens when the driver changes or disappears.

KLAAT test Record two minutes of drone followed by five minutes of pulse. Count Ankora beats between consecutive inhalation starts. Look for a stable ratio that persists for multiple breaths and then observe whether it dissolves after the pulse stops.
