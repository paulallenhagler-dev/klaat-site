01 / The inquiry

Experience first.
Explanation second.

KLAAT begins with two acoustic forces: Amastacia, the C♯ agave didgeridoo, and Ankora, the elk-hide frame drum. Drone, breath, pulse, repetition, and duration generate the experience. The research asks what might explain it.

The book explores resonance and sound physics, rhythm and expectation, attention and absorption, bodily sensation, performer flow, and the different experiences created by live sound and playback.

02 / The evidence boundary

Strange without
being sloppy.

The inquiry separates what the literature supports from what KLAAT has actually demonstrated. That matters: perceptual absorption is not proof of trance induction; a compelling rhythm is not evidence of a universal brain frequency; and a felt bodily response does not identify its cause.

Research draft, not a peer-reviewed monograph.
This is a living synthesis of published science and working hypotheses. KLAAT-specific listening and physiological measurements are still proposed or in early development. No therapeutic outcomes are claimed.
